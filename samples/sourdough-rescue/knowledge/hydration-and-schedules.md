# Hydration and schedules

## Bulk time by temperature
For a dough with 20% starter (baker's percentage), bulk fermentation to 50% growth takes roughly:
- 18C (64F): 9 to 10 hours
- 20C (68F): 6 to 7 hours
- 22C (72F): 5 to 6 hours
- 24C (75F): 4 to 5 hours
- 26C (79F): 3.5 to 4 hours
With 10% starter, add about 2 hours at each temperature.

## Cold retard
After shaping, retard at 3C to 5C (37F to 41F) for 10 to 16 hours. Longer than 18 hours raises the risk of overproofing, especially above 5C.

## Building a schedule backwards
Start from when bread should come out of the oven. Subtract 45 minutes of baking, 45 minutes of preheating (overlapping with the end of retard), the retard window, 30 minutes for shaping and bench rest, bulk time from the table, and 4 to 6 hours for the starter to peak after feeding.

## Hydration by flour
- Supermarket bread flour (12 to 13% protein): 70% to 75%
- All-purpose flour (10 to 11% protein): 65% to 68%
- 20% wholewheat: add 3% to 5% water
- High-extraction or heritage flours: start at 68% and adjust by 2% steps
