# Rescue Matrix: troubleshooting guide

## Flat loaf, gummy crumb
Most often underproofed bulk. At 20C (68F) a 20% starter dough at 75% hydration needs 6 to 7 hours of bulk, not 4. The dough tells you it is ready when it has grown about 50%, shows bubbles on the sides of the container and jiggles as one mass. Contributing cause: slicing before the loaf has cooled for at least 1 hour, which leaves the crumb gummy even when proof was right. One change: extend bulk by 90 minutes at the same temperature.

## Flat loaf, open and lacy crumb
Overproofed. The structure gave out before the bake. Common after a warm day or a long cold retard over 18 hours. The dough tells you: it spreads on the counter when turned out and the poke test springs back not at all. One change: shorten bulk by 1 hour, or end it at 30% growth instead of 50%.

## Dense, tight crumb
Usually a weak or young starter, or flour with low protein. Check the starter first: it should double within 4 to 6 hours of feeding at 24C (75F). If the starter is fine, raise hydration by 5% in steps. One change: use the starter at its peak, not after it has fallen.

## Big tunnels under the crust (flying crust)
The top dried out or shaping left a large air pocket. Also caused by an oven that is too hot on top. One change: cover the shaped loaf during the cold retard and degas gently with a fingertip before final shaping.

## Pale crust
Low oven temperature, too little steam at the start, or an over-fermented dough that used up its sugars. One change: preheat the Dutch oven for 45 minutes at 250C (482F) and bake lid-on for the first 20 minutes.

## Sour or vinegar smell, very sour taste
Long, cool fermentation favours acetic acid. One change: feed the starter at 1:5:5 and keep bulk at 24C to 26C (75F to 79F).

## Starter not doubling
In order of likelihood: kitchen too cold (below 21C), feeding ratio too high for the temperature, chlorinated tap water, flour change to a low-ash white flour. Recovery routine for 3 days: feed 1:2:2 twice a day with 20% wholegrain rye, keep at 24C to 26C, use filtered water. Discard a starter with pink or orange streaks.
